Admissions

Start your academic journey with Forces Academy Faisalabad.

How to Apply

Follow these simple steps to complete your admission process.

1
Choose Your Program

Select the academic program that matches your educational goals.

2
Check Eligibility

Review the eligibility requirements for your selected program.

3
Prepare Documents

Gather the required academic and personal documents.

4
Submit Application

Submit your completed admission application with the required documents.

5
Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation and information about the next steps.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility requirements for our academic programs.

FSc Pre-Medical

Students who have completed Matriculation with a science background and meet the required academic criteria.

FSc Pre-Engineering

Students with a science background who wish to continue their studies in engineering and related fields.

Matric Science

Students seeking secondary-level education with a focus on science subjects.

Matric Arts

Students interested in humanities, social sciences and arts-related subjects.

Entry Test Preparation

Students preparing for university and professional entry examinations.

ICS Computer Science

Students interested in computer science, software development, information technology and related fields.

Required Documents

Applicants should prepare the following documents for admission.

  • Copy of previous academic result or certificate
  • Copy of CNIC or B-Form
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school or college leaving certificate
  • Domicile or other supporting document, if required
